# English texts for the local comparison (crates/parity). Written for jespersen
# and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 with the rest of the
# repository. One text per line; no line breaks inside a text.

# Everyday sentences.
The weather was cold and grey all morning.
She opened the window and listened to the rain.
We walked along the river until the light began to fade.
Could you pass me the salt, please?
I think the train leaves at a quarter past nine.
They bought fresh bread, cheese and a bottle of water.
The children laughed when the dog chased its own tail.
He forgot his umbrella again, so he waited under the bridge.
Please close the door quietly behind you.
My brother lives in a small house near the sea.

# Narrative prose.
The old lighthouse keeper climbed the stairs one last time.
Nobody in the village remembered who had planted the orchard.
At dawn the fishermen pulled their boats onto the sand.
She read the letter twice, then folded it and put it away.
The road wound through the hills like a ribbon of dust.
A single candle burned on the table beside the empty chair.
He promised himself that he would never return to that city.
The library smelled of paper, leather and forgotten afternoons.
They argued about the map until the sun had nearly set.
Somewhere beyond the forest, a bell began to ring.

# Questions and exclamations.
Where did you put the keys?
What a wonderful surprise!
Have you ever seen anything so beautiful?
Why would anyone build a house on such a steep hill?
Stop right there!

# Words that test vowels, stress and flapping.
The butter is better than the margarine.
Water the garden after the party.
A little bottle of metal polish sat on the ladder.
Photography and photographic memory are related.
The record shows that they record every meeting.
Understanding the nature of language takes patience.
Electricity, economics and history are difficult subjects.
The comfortable chair was in the laboratory.
Thirty dirty birds turned over the earth.

# Function words and contractions.
I can't believe it isn't ready yet.
You'd better ask her if she's coming with us.
It was the best of all the things that we had.
There are some of them in the box over there.
What do you want to do with it?
